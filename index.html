Ember Garden

A calm idle tower defense game. A stone lantern in a zen garden holds back waves of intruders while you place shrines, choose upgrades and grow the garden.

Coming soon to Google Play

Calm defense

Your lantern fires on its own. Spend coins from each wave on upgrades and decide where the next shrine goes.

Shrines that evolve

Ember, Lotus, Thorn, Mist, Bell, Heron and Willow shrines, each upgraded one by one and some evolving into stronger forms.

Restless intruders

Swift runners, heavy brutes, swarms, splitters that break apart and menders that heal their allies.

Two ways to play

Classic: one life, and every run is kept with your best wave. Endless: a failed wave starts again, and your garden waits for you between visits.

No ads, no accounts, no tracking

Ember Garden works offline and keeps your progress on your phone. It doesn't collect any personal data. Read the privacy policy.

Support

Found a bug, lost progress or have an idea? Email embergarden.support@gmail.com. Including your phone model and Android version helps.
